Privacy Policy for Ember Survivors

Last updated: July 2026

FizzleGames ("we", "us") operates the mobile game Ember Survivors (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

The App itself does not require account creation or collect personally identifiable information directly. However, the App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google AdMob - used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads.
  • Google Play Billing / Unity In-App Purchasing - used to process in-app purchases. Purchase transactions are handled by Google Play; we do not store your payment information.
  • Unity Gaming Services - used to support in-app purchase functionality.

Local Data Storage

The App stores game progress, settings, and currency balances locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers.
